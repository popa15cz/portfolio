ABOUT ME

I was born in the Czech Republic on September 23, 2009. When I was one year and eight months old, I took my first flight on a commercial aircraft. My passion for aviation began developing from that moment, and airplanes have fascinated me ever since.

During my childhood, I also received my first camera, a Nikon Coolpix L29, which I took with me almost everywhere. Photography gradually became another important part of my interests.

In 2019, I got my first computer and began exploring how computers and software worked. I started by creating and managing Minecraft servers, which gave me my first experience with community management, VPS administration, server infrastructure, and web development. I learned HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and later moved on to Java, where I began developing my own Minecraft plugins and larger server systems.

Around 2022, I discovered the Saitek FIP, a physical display used in flight simulators. I started developing my own gauges and eventually released some of them to the flight simulation community. This became an interesting combination of my interests in aviation, software, and visual design.

In 2025, I became more serious about photography after using a Canon PowerShot G9 and later a Nikon D3200. The additional control offered by a DSLR allowed me to explore photography much more deeply.

After the summer of 2025, I enrolled at the Secondary School of Aviation, where I currently study aircraft mechanics with a focus on avionics. At the same time, I became increasingly interested in full-stack web development. I began working with technologies such as Spring Framework, Docker, MySQL, and PostgreSQL and used them to build an e-commerce platform for my FIP gauges, which I launched in August 2026.

In January 2026, I also began glider pilot training at the Slovácký Aeroclub Kunovice. My practical training is currently ongoing as I work toward completing my licence.

Today, my main interests come together in three areas: aviation, software development, and photography. Although each began differently, they all share the same idea - I enjoy learning how things work, developing new skills, and creating something of my own.

ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES

I have been running my own aviation-focused Discord community for over three years, where I have developed strong communication, moderation and leadership skills.

I also have some experience using Adobe After Effects to create and edit short videos, animations and other visual content.

I have experience working with CNC milling machine and some experience using VCarve Pro.

EDUCATION

I successfully completed primary school before continuing to secondary school.

I am currently in high school at "Střední škola Letecká" in Kunovice, studying aircraft mechanics with a focus on avionics.
