ABOUT ME

I am a 17 year old Czech student with a strong interest in aviation, photography, and software development.

My photography focuses primarily on aviation, although I am also exploring other genres, including portrait photography. I work with Nikon equipment and continuously develop my photographic skills.

In software development, I work mainly with Java, Spring Framework, Docker, and PostgreSQL. I also develop custom flight simulation instruments and gauges through my own project, Just Gauges.

I currently study aircraft mechanics with a specialization in avionics at a secondary aviation school in the Czech Republic. Outside my studies, I am also pursuing glider pilot training at Slovácký Aeroclub Kunovice.

ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCES

I have been running my own aviation-focused Discord community for over three years, where I have developed strong communication, moderation and leadership skills.

I also have some experience using Adobe After Effects to create and edit short videos, animations and other visual content.

I have experience working with CNC milling machine and some experience using VCarve Pro.

EDUCATION

I successfully completed primary school before continuing to secondary school.

I am currently in high school at "Střední škola Letecká" in Kunovice, studying aircraft mechanics with a focus on avionics.
